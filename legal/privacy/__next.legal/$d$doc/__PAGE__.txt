2:T9f9,MIMICORE - PRIVACY POLICY
Version 2026-10-03

SHORT VERSION
Your voice never leaves your computer. All voice processing runs on your PC. We keep only what is needed to
run your account: your email address, your plan and your credit usage.

WHAT STAYS ON YOUR COMPUTER
- Microphone audio is processed in memory while the live voice runs and is not stored.
- When you create a voice, the voice sample, your spoken consent statement and the voice's data are saved in
  %LOCALAPPDATA%\Mimicoreoices.
- Generated speech is saved in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Mimicore\outputs; converted files go where you choose.
- Settings and logs are kept in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Mimicore. Logs contain technical events (device names,
  timings, errors), never audio or the text you type.
- Uninstalling the app does not delete this folder, so your voices are kept. Delete the folder yourself to
  remove everything.

YOUR ACCOUNT (stored by us, hosted on Supabase)
- Email address, sign-in method (Google or email code), plan, Pro end date, credit balance and a log of credit
  changes (amount, type such as "live", "speech", "convert", "ad", and time).
- When you connect the app, the name of your PC (as Windows reports it) is kept for up to 15 minutes to show it
  on the confirmation page.
- Rewarded ads: the time an ad started and finished, to limit ad credits per day.
We never receive your audio, voices, recordings or the text you type. We do not sell personal data.
You can ask us to delete your account at ribanixlabs@zohomail.in.

PAYMENTS
Pro subscriptions are processed by Razorpay, which handles your payment details under its own privacy policy.
We receive only the subscription status and period, linked to your account.

ADS ON THE WEBSITE
If you choose to watch ads to earn credits, the ads are served by Google (AdSense), which may use cookies and
your IP address under Google's own policies. The app itself shows no ads.

WHAT ELSE GOES OVER THE INTERNET
- Model files you install (about 1.1-1.8 GB per quality tier) are downloaded from Hugging Face and GitHub,
  which see your IP address and which files were requested.
- The app contacts our account server to sign in, check your plan and record credit use. If you are offline,
  usage is recorded on your PC and sent the next time you are online.
- Update checks contact GitHub.
- If the Microsoft Edge WebView2 runtime is missing, the installer downloads it from Microsoft.
- Links you click open in your web browser.

CHILDREN
The Software is not intended for anyone under 18.

CONTACT
