VOICEMIMICRY ZERO - PRIVACY POLICY
Version 2026-10-01

SHORT VERSION
Your voice never leaves your computer. There are no accounts, analytics, advertising or telemetry.

WHAT STAYS ON YOUR COMPUTER
- Microphone audio is processed in memory while the live voice runs and is not stored.
- When you create a voice, the voice sample, your spoken consent statement and the voice's data are saved in
  %LOCALAPPDATA%\Mimicore\voices.
- Generated speech is saved in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Mimicore\outputs; converted files go where you choose.
- Settings and logs are kept in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Mimicore. Logs contain technical events (device names,
  timings, errors), never audio or the text you type.
- Uninstalling the app does not delete this folder, so your voices are kept. Delete the folder yourself to
  remove everything.

WHAT GOES OVER THE INTERNET
- Once, on first start (or after a repair): the voice model files (about 1.2 GB) are downloaded from Hugging
  Face (huggingface.co), which hosts Resemble AI's official Chatterbox-Turbo files. Hugging Face sees your IP
  address and which files were requested, as any web server does. Nothing about you, your voice or your
  recordings is sent.
- If the Microsoft Edge WebView2 runtime is missing, the installer downloads it from Microsoft.
- Links you click (for example to install VB-CABLE) open in your web browser.
