MIMICORE - PRIVACY POLICY

Version 2026-10-04

SHORT VERSION

Your voice never reaches us. Live voice, cloning, effects, speech and on-PC training run on your PC. If you choose the optional cloud training, your recordings go only to YOUR OWN Google Drive and a Google Colab session in YOUR OWN Google account, never to our servers. We keep only what is needed to run your account: your email address, your plan and your plan.

WHAT STAYS ON YOUR COMPUTER

  • Microphone audio is processed in memory while the live voice runs and is not stored.
  • When you create a voice, the voice sample, your spoken consent statement and the voice's data are saved in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Mimicore\voices.
  • Generated speech is saved in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Mimicore\outputs; converted files go where you choose.
  • Settings and logs are kept in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Mimicore. Logs contain technical events (device names, timings, errors), never audio or the text you type.
  • Uninstalling the app does not delete this folder, so your voices are kept. Delete the folder yourself to remove everything.

YOUR ACCOUNT (stored by us, hosted on Supabase)

  • Email address, sign-in method (Google, Discord or email code), plan, trial and Pro end dates, how many free live minutes you used today, and a record of your payments (Razorpay payment id, plan, amount and time).
  • When you connect the app, the name of your PC (as Windows reports it) is kept for up to 15 minutes to show it on the confirmation page.

We never receive your audio, voices, recordings or the text you type. We do not sell personal data. You can ask us to delete your account at ribanixlabs@zohomail.in.

PAYMENTS

Pro purchases are processed by Razorpay, which handles your payment details under its own privacy policy. We receive only the payment or subscription status and period, linked to your account.

WHAT ELSE GOES OVER THE INTERNET

  • Model files you install (about 1.1-1.8 GB per quality tier) are downloaded from Hugging Face and GitHub, which see your IP address and which files were requested.
  • The app contacts our account server to sign in, check your plan and record free live minutes. If you are offline, usage is recorded on your PC and sent the next time you are online.
  • Update checks contact GitHub.
  • Cloud training (only if you use it): Google Drive, Google sign-in and Google Colab, as described above.
  • If the Microsoft Edge WebView2 runtime is missing, the installer downloads it from Microsoft.
  • Links you click open in your web browser.

OPTIONAL CLOUD TRAINING (YOUR OWN GOOGLE ACCOUNT)

Only if you choose "Cloud" when training a voice (in the app or on our website):

  • You connect your Google account. Mimicore asks only for Google's "drive.file" permission: it can see, create and delete only the files Mimicore itself creates (the "Mimicore Cloud Training" folder). It cannot see any other file in your Drive, your photos or your email. It also reads your Google email address to show which account is connected.
  • The app or website uploads your recordings directly from your device to that folder in your Drive. They never pass through, and are never stored on, Ribanix Labs servers. We cannot access your Drive: the access token stays on your PC (in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Mimicore\cloud.json) or, on the website, only in your browser tab's memory.
  • Training runs in our open-source notebook (github.com/ribanixlabs/mimicore-app/tree/main/colab) in a Google Colab session under your Google account. Colab asks for its own Google Drive permission; that is Google's service, and the notebook opens only the Mimicore Cloud Training folder. Google deletes the temporary Colab machine when the session ends. Google's own terms and privacy policy apply to Drive and Colab.
  • When the trained voice is installed, Mimicore moves the job folder (recordings, checkpoints, voice) to your Drive trash, unless you chose to keep the recordings. You can delete the folder yourself at any time.
  • Disconnect in Settings or on the website, or remove "Mimicore" at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Mimicore's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not use it for advertising, do not sell it, and no human at Ribanix Labs reads it.

ERROR REPORTS (OFF UNLESS YOU TURN THEM ON)

If you switch on "Send error reports" in Settings, then when Mimicore crashes or hits an unexpected error it sends a short technical report to our server (hosted on Supabase): the error message and where in the code it happened, the app version, the Windows version and a random installation ID that is not linked to your account. Your user name is removed from file paths. Reports never include audio, voices, recordings, the text you type, your email or your account. Reports are kept for up to 90 days and used only to fix bugs. Switch it off at any time.
