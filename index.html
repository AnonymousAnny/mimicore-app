Questions, answered

Is Mimicore really free?

Yes. Every feature is on the free plan, with 30 minutes of voice time every day plus a daily bonus on this website. Voice effects and voice training never use minutes. Pro (₹99/month) removes the limit.

Does my voice get uploaded?

Never to us. Recording, cloning, live conversion, speech and on-PC training all run on your PC. If you choose cloud training, your recordings go only to your own Google Drive and a Colab session in your own Google account: Mimicore can see only the files it creates there, and they're moved to your Drive trash once the voice is installed. We store only your account email, plan and minute balance.

I don't have an NVIDIA graphics card. Can I still train a voice?

Yes. Cloud training uses a free NVIDIA GPU from Google Colab in your own Google account: same trainer, same three quality levels, same result. Start it in the app (Voices › Train a voice › Cloud) or on the Cloud training page. Instant voices and all effects work on any PC without training.

What PC do I need?

Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit). A DirectX 12 graphics card from NVIDIA, AMD or Intel is recommended for live voices; effects run on any PC. Training your own voice needs an NVIDIA GTX 16 / RTX 20 series card or newer with 4 GB+.

How do I use it in Discord, Zoom or games?

Mimicore installs the free VB-CABLE virtual microphone for you with one click. Then pick “CABLE Output” as your microphone in the other app.

Can I clone anyone’s voice?

Only your own voice, or a voice whose owner has given you permission. Mimicore records a spoken consent statement and marks generated files as AI-generated. Impersonation and fraud are forbidden by our Acceptable Use Policy.

How do I cancel Pro?

Any time, from the Razorpay link in your subscription email. Pro keeps working until the end of the paid month.
