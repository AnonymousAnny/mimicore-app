Train your voice in the cloud

No NVIDIA graphics card? Train a studio-grade voice on a free Google GPU. Same trainer and same quality as on a PC, and your recordings stay in your own Google Drive.

  1. 1

    Connect your Drive

    Mimicore gets access only to the files it creates.

  2. 2

    Upload & press ▶

    Your recordings go to your Drive; Colab trains them on a free GPU.

  3. 3

    Use it in Mimicore

    The app installs the finished voice automatically.

🔒 Your voice stays yours

  • Mimicore can see only the files it creates: the Mimicore Cloud Training folder in your Google Drive. Not your other files, photos or email.
  • Ribanix Labs never receives your recordings. This page uploads them from your browser straight to your own Drive. No Mimicore server is involved.
  • Training runs in a Google Colab session in your account; Google deletes that temporary machine when the session ends.
  • When the voice is installed, the recordings and training files are moved to your Drive trash, unless you choose to keep them.
  • The training notebook is open source: anyone can check that it only opens the Mimicore folder.
  • Disconnect any time on this page or at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
